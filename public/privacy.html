Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information CineHall collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. CineHall does not require accounts and does not collect your name, email address, or payment details. We aim to collect as little data as possible.

Summary: We automatically log basic technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) to operate the service and block abuse. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to anyone. Everything else you read below is the detail behind that statement.

1. Information We Collect

  • Server logs (automatic): When you browse CineHall, our servers may log technical information such as your IP address, browser/device type, the pages you request, and timestamps. This is standard web-server logging used to keep the service running, measure traffic, and defend against attacks.
  • Search queries: The keywords you search for are processed to show results and may appear in server logs. Search is performed by CineHall and, for some catalog data, by the third-party sources we integrate with.
  • Local storage: Your browser may store small amounts of data on your device (e.g., theme preference or last-watched position) via browser local storage. This never leaves your device.
  • No account data: CineHall has no user accounts, registrations, or profiles. We cannot identify individual visitors by name.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website and its features.
  • To protect the service from abuse, fraud, and technical attacks.
  • To measure aggregate traffic and understand which content categories are popular, in aggregated (non-identifying) form only.
  • To respond to lawful requests where required by applicable law (see Section 5).

3. Cookies & Local Storage

CineHall itself does not use tracking cookies for advertising. Some features rely on your browser's local storage (a technical mechanism similar to cookies) to remember your preferences on your own device. Third-party services we rely on may set their own cookies — see Section 6.

4. No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, license, or otherwise disclose your personal information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Disclosure to Third Parties

We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: hosting, analytics, and edge-network providers that process data on our behalf under their own security obligations.
  • Legal compliance: when required by law, subpoena, or government request, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

In case of a merger, sale, or transfer of assets, collected data may be transferred as part of that transaction.

6. Third-Party Services

CineHall displays content and uses services operated by third parties, including: video embed/stream providers (which may log your IP and viewing activity on their servers), fonts services, and content databases. These third parties have their own privacy policies, and CineHall has no control over their data practices. When you watch content embedded from a third-party provider, that provider's policy applies to their handling of your data. We encourage you to review their policies.

7. Data Retention & Security

Server logs are retained only as long as reasonably necessary (typically days to weeks) and are routinely purged. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the service and any data we hold against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take industry-standard precautions.

8. Children's Privacy

CineHall is a general-audience service and is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. International Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, you may have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold about you, and the right to object to or restrict certain processing. Because we collect minimal data and do not maintain accounts, these requests can typically be fulfilled by simply contacting us; we will respond within the legally required timeframe. Legal bases for processing include our legitimate interest in operating and securing the service and compliance with legal obligations.

10. Your Choices

  • Clear your browser's cache/local storage to remove on-device preferences.
  • Use your browser's private/incognito mode to reduce local data persistence.
  • Block third-party embeds with browser extensions if you do not wish to interact with third-party providers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be highlighted at the top of this page for at least 30 days.

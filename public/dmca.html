DMCA Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

CineHall respects the intellectual property rights of others and complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512). This page explains how copyright owners can request the removal of infringing content and how users can respond to a removal notice.

Important: CineHall does not host, upload, store, or transmit any video files, images, or copyrighted media on its own servers. All movies, series and streams visible on the site are provided by independent third-party sources (embed/stream providers) over which CineHall has no control. CineHall merely indexes and links to those publicly available sources. If content is removed by the original provider, it disappears from CineHall automatically.

1. Filing a Takedown Notice

If you are a copyright owner (or are authorized to act on behalf of one) and believe content accessible through CineHall infringes your copyright, send a written notice to our designated agent containing all of the following elements, as required by 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3):

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work(s) claimed to have been infringed.
  • Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing — specifically, the exact URL(s) on CineHall where the material is accessible, so we can locate it.
  • Your contact information: full name, mailing address, telephone number, and e-mail address.
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

2. Where to Send the Notice

Send DMCA notices to our designated agent:
Email: dmca@cinehall.xyz
Subject line: DMCA Takedown Notice

3. What Happens Next

Upon receipt of a valid notice, we will promptly review it and, where required, remove or disable access to the identified link(s) — typically within 1–3 business days. We may contact you if additional information is needed to process your request. We also notify the provider of the affected content where appropriate.

4. Counter-Notification

If you believe that content was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, you may file a counter-notification under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3). It must include:

  • Your physical or electronic signature.
  • Identification of the material removed and the URL where it previously appeared.
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  • Your name, address, and telephone number, and a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district in which your address is located (or, if outside the United States, any judicial district in which CineHall may be found).

Send counter-notifications to dmca@cinehall.xyz with the subject DMCA Counter-Notice.

5. Repeat Infringer Policy

In accordance with 17 U.S.C. § 512(i), we maintain a policy of terminating the access of users or linked sources that are determined to be repeat infringers, where reasonably possible.

6. Abuse Warning

Section 512(f) makes anyone who knowingly misrepresents that material is infringing (or was removed by mistake) liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees. Only file a notice or counter-notice if you are certain of your claim. Inquiries that are not valid DMCA notices (e.g., general licensing requests) will not be processed through this channel — contact us via the email above with an appropriate subject instead.

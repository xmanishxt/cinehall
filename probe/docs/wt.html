FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers to the things people ask most.

Is Webtor.io free to use?

Yes, Webtor.io is completely free for basic use. You can stream and download torrents without creating an account. Optional paid plans remove ads, increase bandwidth, and unlock Vault cloud storage.

Do I need a VPN to use Webtor.io? Will my ISP see what I download?

No VPN required. All traffic between you and Webtor.io travels over HTTPS. Your IP address is never exposed to the BitTorrent network because Webtor.io servers handle all peer communication. For your ISP, using Webtor.io looks no different from visiting any other website.

Can I use Webtor.io on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Webtor.io works in any modern mobile browser on iOS and Android. You can stream video, listen to audio, and download torrents without installing any additional apps.

What video and audio formats does Webtor.io support?

Webtor.io supports instant streaming for most popular formats. Video: AVI, MKV, MP4, WEBM, M4V, TS, VOB. Audio: MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, M4A. Files that require transcoding may take a moment longer to start playing.

Is Webtor.io an open-source project?

Yes, 100%. All components of Webtor.io are distributed under the MIT license. You can visit the GitHub repository to view the source code and contribute.

Can I watch my library on a smart TV?

Yes! With the Stremio integration you can stream your personal library on any smart TV or device running Stremio. Just install the addon using the link from your profile.

How do I open a .torrent file in my browser?

Drag your .torrent file into the upload area at the top of this page, or paste a magnet link into the search field. Webtor opens it instantly — pick the video or audio file you want and stream it, or grab a direct download link. There's no torrent client to install and nothing to set up.

What can I use to open a torrent file?

You don't need to install anything — Webtor opens torrent files right in your browser on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, and iOS. Upload the .torrent file or paste a magnet link, then stream the video or save the files over a regular HTTPS download. Desktop clients like qBittorrent or uTorrent also work, but they require installation and connect your device to the torrent swarm.

How do I download files from a magnet link without a torrent client?

Paste the magnet link into the search field at the top of this page. Webtor acts as an online magnet link downloader: it runs the magnet download in the cloud and gives you a direct HTTP download link for each file, plus a built-in player to stream video and audio. Your browser never connects to the BitTorrent network — Webtor servers handle that.

How do I use a magnet link?

Paste the magnet link into the search field at the top of this page — Webtor opens it in seconds, with no client to install. You can also drag and drop a .torrent file instead. Webtor fetches the data on its own servers, so your browser never joins the BitTorrent swarm; you simply stream or download the files.

How do I download torrented files without a torrent client?

Paste a magnet link or upload a .torrent file at the top of this page. Webtor fetches the torrent on its servers and gives you a direct HTTPS download link for every file — nothing to install, and it works on any device. Your browser never joins the BitTorrent swarm, so no open ports or client setup are needed.

How do I unzip a torrent file?

A .torrent file is not an archive, so there is nothing to unzip — it's a small metadata file that only describes the real content. To get the actual files, open the .torrent with Webtor and download them one by one, or use the Torrent to ZIP tool to download everything as a single ZIP or TAR archive.

What is Discover and how do the AI recommendations work?

Discover is a recommendations page powered by AI. Open it from the main navigation, type what you want to watch (or tap one of the suggested chips), and Webtor returns a personalized list of films — each with a short reason explaining why it fits your taste and what you've watched before. Click any card to start streaming through your usual addons.

Is AI Discover free? How many requests do I get?

Yes — every account gets 2 free AI request(s) per day. Supporters of the project get 15 requests per day. Suggestion chips and the rest of the Discover catalog are unlimited; only the natural-language search counts against the daily quota.

What AI model powers the recommendations? Is my data safe?

Recommendations are generated by Anthropic's Claude — a fast model for the free tier and a more capable one for supporters. We send the model only your title/year/rating history. No email, IP address, or any other personal data is shared. The response is used purely to suggest films you can stream through your usual addons.

Do download links or streams expire?

Yes, for security reasons direct download links and streaming sessions are temporary and may expire after a short time. You can always refresh or regenerate them from your library interface.

How can I remove ads on Webtor.io?

Support the project and you'll get an ad-free experience, faster loading speeds, and a heart icon in the interface.

Why do some downloaded ZIP archives show a CRC error?

ZIP archives are generated on-the-fly and don't include full checksum data, so some extraction tools may show a CRC warning even when the file is intact. That's why directory downloads now default to TAR — the TAR format has no per-file checksums, so even an interrupted and resumed download unpacks cleanly. If you prefer ZIP, pick it from the arrow menu next to the download button and use 7-Zip for best compatibility.

Why is my download or streaming speed slow on Webtor.io?

Speeds may vary due to the number of seeders, file format (some require real-time transcoding), your internet connection, current server load, and your account type. Premium users get higher bandwidth.

Note: bandwidth is measured in Mbps (megabits per second), while file sizes are shown in MB (megabytes). 1 MB = 8 Mb, so a 50 Mbps connection gives roughly 6.25 MB/s download speed. Supporting the project helps unlock better speed and reliability.
